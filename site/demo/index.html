When should a router step back?

Twelve requests come in. Readers suggest a desk and say how sure they are. The harness either answers or hands the request to a person, and writes down why. Start with the case it gets wrong.

    Support is the confidence-weighted share of readers naming a desk. Below the threshold, the request goes to a person. A second gate also holds it back if the expected cost of a wrong answer reaches 0.3, the cost of asking a person. Opens at 0.50: three answered, six held back for low support, three for cost, and M11 still slips through.
